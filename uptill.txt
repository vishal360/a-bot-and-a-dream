2. THE GOAL
-----------
Build a local-first trading workspace that an operator can understand, inspect
and control, initially using paper money and real public market observations.
Eventually investigate agent-assisted or agentic trading, but only after the
manual experience, accounting, execution simulation and risk boundaries have
earned trust. This is not an assertion that an AI strategy is profitable.

The immediate user priority is a coherent manual paper-trading terminal:
chart, order entry, holdings, average prices, P&L and execution history together.
The user rejected the fragmented interface and wanted this redesigned before
we proceeded to agentic trading. The manual workspace must be accepted first.

Longer-term direction:
* Research reproducible strategies on appropriate, provenance-verified data.
* Compare realistic results with baselines and untouched evaluation windows.
* Paper-forward-test with conservative execution and independent risk controls.
* Introduce isolated agents that propose structured intents, not unrestricted
  broker orders or arbitrary edits to production strategy code.
* Consider real venue execution only as a separate, explicitly approved project
  with authentication, reconciliation, failure drills and readiness evidence.

3. CONVERSATION AND DEVELOPMENT HISTORY
---------------------------------------
A. Initial terminal and research foundation
The earlier implementation created a science-fiction-inspired React terminal,
local Bun server, SQLite persistence, research backtesting, bot profiles and
persistent risk controls. Profiles include ATLAS (MES futures research) and
ORION (BTC-USD crypto research). Names/profiles do not mean deployed strategies.

The research engine is a deterministic long-only channel breakout: a completed
bar breaking the previous N highs creates a next-open entry. Exits use a fixed
stop, completed-bar lower-channel signal at the next open, or final close.
Gaps through stops execute at the worse of stop/open before adverse slippage.
Futures quantities are whole contracts; MES research uses multiplier 5.
Uploaded OHLC and explicitly labeled synthetic inputs support research plumbing.
Neither synthetic nor unverified uploaded results establish trading edge.

6. EXECUTION MODEL AND WHY IT IS CONSERVATIVE
--------------------------------------------
Buy IOC consumes visible asks; sell IOC consumes visible bids. Matching uses
venue precision/minimums and a 50-basis-point price collar, after modeled delay.
Insufficient liquidity means partial fill and cancellation of the remainder.
Mark/index values are not used as unlimited executable liquidity. There is no
synthetic-feed fallback if public depth is unavailable or invalid.

Configured assumptions include a 2-basis-point fee per side and 300 ms delay
plus request duration. Those are simulator assumptions, not certified venue
terms. Lighter tier/fee and latency assumptions must be pinned and calibrated
before stronger conclusions about execution realism.

REST snapshots are not sequenced exchange events. The same visible liquidity
can appear on repeated requests without proof it replenished. Consumption is
therefore persisted by market/side/price across refresh/restart; repeated REST
liquidity is not automatically reusable. This may deliberately underfill until
sequenced replenishment is implemented. It is safer than fabricating capacity.

Observations and executed depth evidence persist. Unreferenced raw observations
have a rolling 1,000-record bound, while observations supporting executed orders
are retained. Pending orders become rejected after restart rather than replayed.
Recovery scans all pending records, not only a UI display limit. Manual trading
reopens paused, so restart never silently reactivates exposure.

7. ACCOUNTING DEFINITIONS AND IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS
----------------------------------------------------
Manual-account settlement uses BTC atoms (1e-8 BTC), quote micro-dollars and
BigInt arithmetic. Versioned settlement strings are stored with fills atomically.
Cash, inventory, cost basis, realized P&L and valuation share ledger replay.
Legacy records are not silently rewritten: historical quote/fee values are
rounded to micro-dollars during replay and counted as legacy fills.

Partial closes allocate cost basis/fees and retain the integer remainder until
final close, so rounding does not lose micro-dollars. Corrupt settlements,
oversells and negative-cash histories fail closed rather than invent balances.

Average buy/sell: sum(quantity * execution price) / sum(quantity), separately
for each side across fills; fees excluded. These describe execution history.
Position average entry: remaining inventory cost basis, not the historical
average of every buy after some inventory has been closed.
Closed trade metrics include allocated fees; partial exits are closed legs,
not necessarily complete round-trip trades. Realized equity/drawdown history
does not claim to reconstruct historical unrealized marked-to-market equity.
Daily aggregation is UTC. Imported accounts are separate and can have incomplete
inventory/deposit history; their curves are not certified broker NAV.

8. RISK AND SECURITY BOUNDARIES
-------------------------------
New manual buys require explicit enablement, positive allocation, sufficient
cash, valid depth, and compliance with order/account limits and halt policy.
Selling only reduces existing BTC; it cannot open a short. Reducing while
paused/halted is allowed so a safety stop does not trap existing exposure.
Loss checks run on observations and inside fill transactions, including fees.
The loss latch persists; routine reset is not a bypass. Its daily baseline is
first observed UTC-day equity, not independently verified midnight equity.

ExecutionRisk is a distinct internal foundation: transactional shared budgets,
durable reservations, integer comparisons, connection generation/freshness,
immutable daily baselines and persistent cash-flow-adjusted loss controls.
The documented general ceilings include 20x leverage and 6% daily loss, but
these do NOT mean the manual simulator supports leverage or that live risk is
operational. The manual simulator remains 1x. Full connected reconciliation,
baseline provenance and reservation lifecycle remain incomplete.

The service binds loopback and has no multi-user authentication. Same-origin,
host validation and CSP reduce local web attack surfaces but do not replace
authentication. Do not publish it through tunnels or reverse proxies.
Secrets are AES-256-GCM encrypted in SQLite; data/vault.key is local and ignored.
A backup of both database and key can decrypt secrets. OS access control and
local-machine security still matter. Ingestion tokens are stored as hashes,
shown at generation and revocable by rotation.

Statistics archives are transactional local records, not independent backups.
Open positions block destructive history reset; mainnet simulation fills also
restrict reset to preserve cash and baseline continuity. Session archival needs
more work. Halt never claims to cancel/flatten real broker or external-bot orders.

9. WHY THE INTERFACE WAS REDESIGNED
-----------------------------------
Reliable accounting and risk controls were necessary; scattering basic trading
across pages was not. The operator should be able to answer in one place:
What is the market? What do I hold? What is my cost? What is my P&L? Can I trade?
What happened to my last order? What budget is left? Why was an order rejected?

The redesign brought those answers together while retaining honest semantics:
* Recorded observations instead of fabricated candlesticks.
* Realized equity instead of invented historical unrealized performance.
* Remaining position cost separated from all-fill execution averages.
* Estimated budget labeled indicative, with final fresh-depth checks authoritative.
* Paper mode, halt state and simulation limitations kept distinct from live trading.

PaperAccount.tsx was recreated after an initial combined delete/add patch failed;
separate deletion and recreation succeeded. The surrounding shell/navigation,
paper session payload, analytics, CSS and browser assertions were updated too.
